We met in the most unexpected way: on a blind date. If it hadn't been a blind date, maybe Gloria would've second-guessed it. But luckily, she decided to listen to our mutual best friend, who forced us to go out. There was very little texting beforehand, just diving straight into an unexpectedly perfect first meeting.

Even though Gloria is usually the shyer of the two and takes a long time to get comfortable with anyone, she surprisingly warmed up right away. She led the entire conversation, while Shannon was busy blushing the whole time. We immediately hit it off. Over time, our relationship just kept getting stronger, deeper, and childishly pure, and before long, we became inseparable. Shannon found someone who would laugh at all his pathetic jokes, and Gloria found someone she could be her truest, most childish self with.

And now, we are so excited to finally begin our next chapter: two kids turning into husband and wife.

This is more than just a celebration for us. We want this day to be truly intimate, surrounded by the people we deeply love, celebrating in a place we truly love as well. We cannot wait to share our special day with you. You have been a part of our life's journey in one way or another, and we would now love for you to witness our lives becoming one. Your presence means the world to us, and we are so excited to make some awesome memories with all of you!

With all our love, Gloria and Shannon

Additional Information

Ceremony

The Roce Ceremony is a traditional Goan Catholic ritual of blessing, held on the evening of 18 December. The Wedding Reception on 19 December is a formal sit-down dinner and dance at the same resort. Both events will be outdoors with covered areas available.

Attire

We invite you to dress in smart-casual or semi-formal attire for both evenings. Given the beachside setting, flat or block-heeled footwear is strongly advised. Ladies, a dupatta or light shawl may be handy for the evening breeze.

Transport

Montego Bay Beach Village is approximately 45 minutes from Goa International Airport and 90 minutes from Panaji. We recommend booking a pre-arranged taxi or hiring a local driver for the duration of your stay. Ola and Uber are also available in the area.

Frequently Asked Questions

General Wedding Details

When and where is the wedding?

The Roce Ceremony is on 18 December 2026 and the Wedding Reception is on 19 December 2026. Both events are at Montego Bay Beach Village, Vithaldas Waddo, Morjim, North Goa, 403512.

Do I need to attend both days?

Not at all! You are welcome to attend one or both events. Please indicate your preference on your RSVP form so we can plan accordingly.

What is the dress code?

Smart-casual to semi-formal. We encourage guests to wear vibrant colours and beach-friendly footwear. Please avoid all-white outfits out of courtesy to the bride.

Are children welcome?

Yes, absolutely! We love your little ones and welcome children at both events. Please do let us know on the RSVP if you are bringing children so we can ensure appropriate seating.

What is the RSVP deadline?

We kindly ask that you RSVP by 30 November 2026 so we can finalise catering and seating arrangements.

Will there be a shuttle or transport service?

We are arranging limited shuttle services from select hotels. Details will be shared closer to the date. We also recommend booking a local driver for convenience.

Food & Drinks

Will there be vegetarian options?

Yes, both the Roce dinner and the Reception will have a full vegetarian menu alongside non-vegetarian options. Please mention any dietary requirements on your RSVP.

Is there an open bar?

Yes! An open bar with a selection of cocktails, wine, beer, and soft drinks will be available throughout both evenings. We encourage you to celebrate responsibly and arrange a safe ride.

Can I request a special meal?

If you have specific allergies or dietary needs, please note them on your RSVP or get in touch with us directly. We will do our best to accommodate you.

What cuisine will be served?

A mix of Goan, Indian, and Continental cuisine is planned across both days — with a special emphasis on local seafood and traditional Goan favourites for the Roce dinner.

Accommodation & Travel

Is accommodation available at the venue?

Montego Bay Beach Village does have on-site accommodation. We recommend booking directly with the resort and mentioning the wedding group. A limited number of rooms are being held.

How do I get to Morjim from the airport?

Goa International Airport (Dabolim) is approximately 45 minutes away by taxi. The new Manohar International Airport (Mopa) is about 20 minutes. Pre-booked taxis and Ola/Uber are all readily available.

What are the nearest hotels?

Please see the "Where to Stay" section above for our curated list of nearby hotels and properties. We will update with block-booking details as they are confirmed.

Is parking available at the venue?

Yes, Montego Bay Beach Village has on-site parking available for guests. If you are staying nearby, a short taxi or auto-rickshaw ride is the most convenient option.
