A letter from us to you

We met on a blind date set up by our best friend Rebecca, who had already decided we were perfect for each other.

Gloria, usually the shy one, led the whole conversation while Shannon sat there blushing. Apparently, it was her best decision ever, because Shannon is cool, handsome, and so much fun. Gloria said that. Shannon didn’t type this.

Somewhere between his lame jokes and her goofy side, our relationship kept getting deeper until we became inseparable. Now, two overgrown kids are becoming husband and wife.

This is more than a celebration for us. We want it to be truly intimate, with only the people we deeply love, in a place we love too. You’ve been part of our journey, and we’d love for you to witness our lives becoming one. Your presence means the world to us, and we can’t wait to make awesome memories with you!

With all our love, Gloria and Shannon

Frequently Asked Questions

General Wedding Details

When and where is the wedding?

The Roce is on 18 December at Montego Bay Beach Village, Morjim. On 19 December, the Nuptial Mass is at 4:00 PM at Our Lady of the Sea Church, Oxel, and the reception follows that evening back at Montego Bay. We would love to have you for all of it.

The Nuptial Mass is at 4:00 PM on 19 December at Our Lady of the Sea Church, Oxel, and the reception follows that evening at Montego Bay, Morjim. We would love to have you there.

Do I need to attend both days?

Not at all. You are welcome for one day or both. Just tell us on your RSVP, so we can plan with you in mind.

What is the dress code?

For the Roce, Indo-Western festive wear. For the reception, formals. Please leave all-white to the bride.

Formals for the reception. Please leave all-white to the bride.

Are children welcome?

Yes, we would love to have them with us. Please add them on your RSVP so we can save them a seat.

Yes, we would love to have them at the reception. Please add them on your RSVP so we can save them a seat.

What is the RSVP deadline?

Please let us know by 15 November 2026. It helps us plan the food and the seats, and we would love to count you in.

Will there be a shuttle or transport service?

Yes. A shuttle will run from Montego Bay to the church and back on the reception day, so the evening is easy to get to.

How do I get there from outside Goa?

Montego Bay is in Morjim, North Goa. A taxi from any of these will get you there.

  • Airport. Manohar International, Mopa (about 40 min), is closest. Dabolim (about 2 hrs) is farther south.
  • Train. Pernem (about 30 min) is nearest. Thivim (about 45 min) has more trains.
  • Bus. Mapusa (about 40 min) or Panaji (about 1 hr).
