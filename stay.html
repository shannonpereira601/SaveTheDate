How prices work

A hotel price is per room for 2 adults. A range runs from the cheapest room to the most expensive room listed.

An Airbnb price is for the whole place. The listing says how many people it sleeps.

Prices include taxes and fees and were checked on 29 October 2026. Distances are from Montego Bay Beach Village. Every place has 100 or more reviews.

Ratings with a star are out of 5. Hotel stars are Google. Airbnb stars are Airbnb’s own. Orange Villa is a Booking.com score, shown out of 10.

Aloha by the Sea is booked by phone. Ask for Ashay and mention Shannon’s name.

Good to know

  • Sharing with friends? A 2–3 bedroom Airbnb split four ways often works out cheaper per person than a hotel room.
  • More than a 15-minute walk away? Plan a cab or scooter, especially for getting back late after the reception.
  • Staying in Siolim? You cross the Siolim bridge, which gets busy in December, so leave a little extra time.
  • Airbnbs are single homes. Once someone books one, it is gone.
  • Different dates? The links open with 17–20 December already selected. Change them if you are staying other nights.
